# CertPath: daily practice for technical certifications
Version 2 · 24 October 2026 · Working title

## Product decision
Build a playful daily lesson path around a rigorous, versioned exam question bank. Duolingo informs the habit loop; Pocket Prep informs exam practice, explanations and domain-level progress. The app’s primary action is **Start today’s five-question lesson**. AI appears after an answer when a learner needs a different explanation.

The outcome is: **know what to practice today, correct a weak concept, and see what to work on next before exam day.** Passing is the eventual customer goal. The product cannot responsibly promise a pass, a fixed preparation time, or an accurate pass probability without outcome evidence.

The launch hypothesis is working professionals preparing for Databricks Data Engineer Associate, with AWS and Microsoft Fabric as later exam banks. A single reviewed pilot is more achievable than launching three thin AI-generated catalogs. This is a scope choice, not proof that Databricks is the best commercial niche.

## What changes from the earlier concept
| Earlier direction | Revised direction |
|---|---|
| Open an AI voice examiner | Open a visible daily learning path |
| Chat drives the whole study session | Short structured question sessions drive learning |
| Reasoning capture on every answer | Optional explanation or reasoning when useful |
| A dramatic “AI exposed my gap” demo | A repeatable “I practiced, understood, and returned” loop |
| Readiness implied by one conversation | Objective coverage, unseen-item accuracy and mock results reported separately |
| Broad certification catalog at launch | One pilot bank; two additional provider paths planned |

## Useful combination
A five-question lesson is easy to start. Questions have reviewed rationales and sources. Mistakes enter a concept review queue. A later fresh scenario checks transfer. A full mock follows the selected exam guide. That sequence makes deciding what to study feel easy; the learning itself still requires effort.

This combination is a positioning hypothesis, not a claim of competitive exclusivity. Pocket Prep already offers multiple quiz modes and an explanation feature. A generic AI chat tab is not a defensible differentiator.

## Proposed pilot
- One exam version, an objective map, six initial daily units, and short lessons.
- Proposed initial content target: 300 original reviewed question versions, including protected mock inventory and multiple scenarios per important objective. The count is a planning estimate; coverage and quality determine release, not the number alone.
- Protect two blueprint-balanced mock forms where the bank supports them. Do not label a repeated daily-question set a fresh mock.
- Free: daily lesson, corrections, due reviews, basic progress. Plus hypothesis: full bank, custom/timed sets, full mocks and bounded AI help.
- No forced social network, public XP league, hearts that stop studying, exam dumps, guaranteed pass claim, or auto-renewing no-card trial.

## Evidence and open questions
Primary product sources support the mechanics, not our demand or retention uplift. The user-supplied Duolingo transcript is an idea source with mixed historical numbers. No customer interviews, willingness-to-pay study, pass-outcome cohort or live traffic experiment has been run for CertPath.

Before a native build, recruit 8–12 relevant candidates and observe how they currently choose study material, interpret mistakes and judge readiness. Test whether a five-question path solves a meaningful problem versus an ordinary practice bank. Ask for an actual paid commitment only after a reviewable offer exists.

The central unknown is whether structure and review create more learning and useful return visits than a practice bank alone. The pilot should measure that directly.
