# Research review
Checked 24 October 2026. Public primary sources support product observations; CertPath demand and effectiveness remain untested.

## Pocket Prep
Pocket Prep presents exam-focused question banks, detailed explanations, performance tracking and modes including Quick 10, Weakest Subject, Missed Questions, Timed, Custom, Level Up and Mock for supported exams. Its September 2026 help article also describes explaining an answer. Therefore “AI explanations” or “multiple practice modes” should not be claimed as our unique invention.

Our Quick 5 is a deliberately smaller lesson-size hypothesis. The proposed daily path, versioned technical objective map and concept review loop are a combination to test, not proof of a vacant market.

Sources:
- https://www.pocketprep.com/
- https://help.pocketprep.com/en/articles/14548723-quiz-modes-explained-how-to-study-with-pocket-prep

## Duolingo
Duolingo’s own streak explanation supports making a repeated action visible and giving users flexibility. It does not prove the same mechanism will improve certification outcomes or transfer the same uplift into this product.

The supplied transcript’s business numbers refer to different periods and definitions. Duolingo’s FY2025 shareholder letter reports 2025 revenue of 1,037.6 million USD and Q4 average DAU of 52.7 million. It reports net income of 414.1 million USD that includes a one-time tax benefit of 256.7 million USD. Treating that net income as a recurring app margin would be misleading. Its 2026 strategy also discusses reducing monetization friction and investing in the free experience.

DAU/MAU is an activity frequency ratio, not same-day return, retention probability or D7 cohort retention. The transcript’s exact organic-growth mix, experiment counts, peer comparisons and social-lift percentages were not established here and are not used as targets.

## Technical exam content
The Databricks Data Engineer Associate public exam page/guide supplies the 45-question, 90-minute format and domain outline used in the prototype. The imported reference snapshot uses weights 6/21/22/16/10/10/15. Before publishing a live bank, recheck the active guide and effective date; the package date is our snapshot date, not an official guide edition label.

The five original illustrative questions concern Delta history, time travel and VACUUM. Microsoft’s Databricks documentation supports the distinction between history/log retention and availability of the underlying data files. These items are not official or recalled exam questions and have not undergone independent subject-matter review. Database seed keeps them draft.
